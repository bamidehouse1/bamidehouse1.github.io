01 / ABOUT ME

Public health research, community engagement, and evidence for change.

I am a microbiologist and Master of Public Health (MPH) student specialising in Medical Demography at the University of Ibadan, Nigeria. My academic background and practical experience have shaped my interest in public health research, population health, and the social factors that influence health and wellbeing.

My field research experience includes working as a Field Research and Data Enumeration Officer with Fairaction Nigeria on the Oyo State Water Poverty Mapping Project and as a Research Assistant on Transil's Strengthening Women's Economic Empowerment Project (SWEEP). These roles involved community data collection, household surveys, focus group discussions, key informant interviews, GPS-tagged data collection, research documentation, and ethical research practices.

I also work as a No Means No Programme Facilitator with APIN Public Health Initiatives, supporting adolescent education, community mobilisation, stakeholder engagement, gender-based violence prevention, programme monitoring, and reporting.

My interests include epidemiology, medical demography, gender-based violence prevention, and research addressing public health and social development challenges. I am developing my skills in R and statistical analysis to strengthen my ability to manage, analyse, and interpret health data. My goal is to contribute to impactful research and public health programmes through reliable fieldwork, evidence generation, and informed decision-making.
